kept unedited for design history: they explain why things were built the way
they were, and they hold detailed specifications for work that is still
deferred.

**They are not current.** Unchecked boxes here do not mean the work is still
planned, and some checked work has since changed. The current plan is
[ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md); shipped changes are in
[CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md). Relative links inside these files were
written for their original locations and may not resolve.
